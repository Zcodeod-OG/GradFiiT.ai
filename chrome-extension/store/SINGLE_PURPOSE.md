# Single purpose (Chrome Web Store)

Paste into the **Single purpose description** field:

---

GradFiT provides virtual clothing try-on on e-commerce and fashion websites. The extension detects product images on pages you visit, lets you preview how garments look on you using your GradFiT account photo, and optionally highlights items that may match your saved closet style. It does not change unrelated page content or collect data for purposes outside fashion try-on and shopping assistance.
