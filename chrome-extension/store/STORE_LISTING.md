## Detailed description

GradFiT helps you see how clothes look on you before you buy them online.

**Try on from any store**
Hover a product image on your favorite fashion sites and tap **Try on**. GradFiT opens a sidebar with a quick AI preview using your GradFiT profile photo.

**Works where you shop**
The extension runs on retailer websites so you do not need to download product images or leave the page.

**Closet-aware recommendations**
When you save items in GradFiT, we can highlight products on other sites that may match your style.

**Combo Studio**
Stage a top from one site and a bottom from another, then try the full look in one shot.

**Buy this (optional)**
After a try-on, open the retailer product page through transparent affiliate links where configured.

**Sign in**
Create a free account at https://gradfit.tech and sign in once. Your session syncs to the extension for full try-on features.
